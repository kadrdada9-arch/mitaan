Anika’s Story: Hope for a Brave Little Fighter

Anika Sharma is a courageous 3-year-old diagnosed with Spinal Muscular Atrophy Type 2. This genetic condition weakens her muscles, makes everyday movement difficult, and urgently requires specialized treatment to help her breathe stronger, move easier, and secure a future.

SMA Type 2 affects the nerve cells that control muscles. Today, Anika depends on her family, medical care, and compassionate support to access the advanced therapy she desperately needs.

Your donation delivers life-changing care, verified hospital support, and transparent fund usage to ensure every contribution helps Anika keep fighting.

What is SMA Type 2?

A serious genetic disorder that causes muscle weakness and respiratory risk. Early treatment improves mobility and quality of life.
